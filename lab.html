04 · METHOD & LIMITS

A calculator, not a training forecast.

The simulator is intentionally transparent. It is useful for orders of magnitude, but real runs differ because active-parameter approximations omit attention and embedding costs, hardware utilization varies, MoE adds communication, and very large clusters do not scale linearly.
