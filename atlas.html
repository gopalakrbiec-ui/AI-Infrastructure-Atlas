03 · WHY CONCENTRATE?

Because wall-clock time is a research variable.

If a training run consumes 30 million accelerator-hours, 15,000 accelerators imply roughly 83 days at perfect scaling; 150,000 imply roughly 8 days. Real scaling is worse than linear, but the incentive remains enormous.

01

Faster iteration

Research is a loop, not a single run. Shorter training cycles let teams test more data recipes, architectures and objectives per year.

  • Hypothesis → train → evaluate
  • Fix failures before competitors
  • Keep model launches on product cadence
02

MoE routing

Sparse experts reduce arithmetic per token, but expert parallelism produces intense all-to-all communication between accelerator groups.

  • Compute falls
  • Routing pressure rises
  • Network quality matters more
03

System fungibility

Large clusters can be reallocated between pretraining, RL, synthetic data generation, evaluation and inference instead of serving one purpose forever.

  • Pretrain today
  • Serve tomorrow
  • Keep utilization economically high
